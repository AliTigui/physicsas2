Index vault-assets

Generate vault.json for the viewer. No install, no build — the page reads your notes locally and never uploads anything.

  1. Copy your Obsidian notes and attachments into the site's vault-assets/ folder.
  2. Open this page from the same site and choose that folder below.
  3. Go back to the viewer and refresh.

Select all files in your vault (webkitdirectory). The page will offer vault.json for download — save it inside vault-assets/.
